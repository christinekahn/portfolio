A podcast for tech professionals — real stories from people who deliver at work and dream beyond it. Host Christine Kahn talks with guests who are navigating tech careers, AI, layoffs and career pivots, and building something of their own alongside (or after) the day job.

Christine Kahn. By day, she's a staff technical program manager in big tech. By night, she's a musician (recording as Christine K) and a sound bath meditation practitioner, based in Portland, OR.
