FAQ

Common questions about the show, the guests, and how it gets made.

What is Dreams and Delivery about?

A podcast for tech professionals — real stories from people who deliver at work and dream beyond it. Host Christine Kahn talks with guests who are navigating tech careers, AI, layoffs and career pivots, and building something of their own alongside (or after) the day job.

Who's the host?

Christine Kahn. By day, she's a staff technical program manager in big tech. By night, she's a musician (recording as Christine K) and a sound bath meditation practitioner, based in Portland, OR.

How often do new episodes come out?

Season 2 is underway, with new episodes roughly every two weeks (Season 1 ran 8 weekly episodes in June–July 2026). New episodes show up in the carousel above automatically as soon as they're published.

How do you pick guests?

Mostly through Christine's own network — former colleagues, people she's met while building the podcast, and referrals from past guests. The common thread is someone balancing a tech career with something they're building, creating, or reinventing outside of it.

Can I be a guest, or suggest one?

Yes — reach out through the contact page with a bit about your story and why it fits the show.

What's the setup — how is the show recorded and published?

Episodes are recorded remotely (Riverside FM), hosted and distributed via Buzzsprout, and the episode cards on this page are pulled live from the show's RSS feed.

Are transcripts available?

Yes, for most episodes — see Episode Transcripts below. They're being added episode by episode, so a few of the newer ones are still in progress.

Where can I listen and subscribe?
